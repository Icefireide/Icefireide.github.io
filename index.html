About

Esports producer and technical director. Most of my work is collegiate and sub-tier-2 competitive gaming: leagues, tournaments and finals weekends where the crew is small and the producer is also directing, switching and troubleshooting live.

I also build the software my broadcasts run on. Graphics control, draft and map-ban displays, roster and stats overlays pulled out of the game’s API, all built around the show instead of bent out of a template, and built so one person can actually run them mid-broadcast. It’s the part of this job I enjoy most, and it’s why my shows tend to look like they had a bigger crew than they did.

Right now I’m producing overnight English-language coverage of the LPL alongside collegiate Valorant, League and Rocket League. Based in the Los Angeles area, remote by default, and looking to do more on-site work.
